UDL Class Planner

For secondary teachers. Start with your pupils' needs: the planner finds what the whole class needs in common, then what individual pupils need, and builds both into your scheme of work.

Files are read in this browser and never uploaded. Pupil information is kept only in this browser until you clear it. Use initials, not names, and check with your school's data protection lead before using real plans. Based on CAST's Universal Design for Learning Guidelines 3.0 and the SEND Code of Practice.
